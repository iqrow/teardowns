---
term: Player mental model
aka: [what the player thinks the system is]
kind: concept
plain: The player’s mental model is a working explanation of how the game works, built from perception, prior knowledge, expectations and interaction.
does: Lets the player predict, plan and make choices without seeing the implementation.
ask: What does the player believe will happen before they commit?
