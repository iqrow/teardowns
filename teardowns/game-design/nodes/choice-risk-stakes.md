---
term: Risk and stakes
aka: ["what might go wrong, and how much it matters"]
kind: knob
plain: Risk concerns uncertain outcomes; stakes are the magnitude of the gain or loss. The same chance of success can feel very different when failure costs a little health versus the whole run.
does: Controls how much uncertainty the player is being asked to accept for a potential benefit.
ask: What are success and failure worth, and what can the player do to change the odds?
range: Set outcome magnitudes and, where the rules use chance, the probability distribution; separately account for unknown opponent actions.
trouble: A supposedly risky option has no meaningful downside, or the player cannot connect catastrophic losses to a choice.
