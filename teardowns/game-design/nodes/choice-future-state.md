---
term: Integrated outcome
aka: [the result matters later]
kind: knob
plain: "An integrated outcome changes the larger course of play: resources, position, relationships, progress or future actions. Set which differences persist and when they expire; integration does not require a permanent story branch."
does: Makes a local choice part of a continuing game rather than an isolated effect.
ask: Which future possibilities change because of this action, and for how long?
cues:
  - Resources persisting after an action
  - Position carried into the next choice
  - Future actions changed by the result
range: Specify the affected state values, duration or reset boundary, and downstream rules that read them.
trouble: Both alternatives immediately reconverge with identical relevant state, or a persistent penalty matters but is never communicated.
