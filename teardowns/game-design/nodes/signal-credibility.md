term: Signal credibility
aka: [would a type really send this, receiver best response]
kind: measure
plain: "Signal credibility is an incentive-compatibility test: each sender type must prefer its signal given receiver beliefs and response, while the receiver responds sequentially rationally. Differential cost is one possible separating mechanism, not a necessary or sufficient test by itself. [MODEL ASSUMPTION]"
does: Tests whether a signal supports a separating or pooling equilibrium under the stated beliefs and best responses, rather than treating publicity or drama as credibility.
ask: For each type, would sending this signal beat its alternatives under the receiver's response, and what belief makes that response rational?
range: Pooling equilibrium; separating equilibrium; semi-separating equilibrium; off-path belief or refinement
trouble: If a proposed signal is affordable to every type, its cost is hidden, or receiver beliefs do not make the response sequentially rational, the observed action need not separate types. [INFERENCE]
