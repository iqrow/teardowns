---
term: Narrative choice
aka: [player choice, branching choice]
kind: knob
plain: A narrative choice offers alternatives whose selection changes the story state, what the player learns, or what can happen next.
does: Turns agency into authored variation and gives players a reason to read, decide and replay.
ask: What does each option reveal, change or commit the player to?
cues:
  - Alternatives in dialogue or story
  - Story state changing after selection
  - New content or outcome after the choice
range: Reflective, tactical or strategic; local or downstream; direct branch, delayed branch or rejoin.
trouble: Choices are cosmetic, all options converge without acknowledgement, or consequences arrive too late to understand.
at: [0.2, 0.25, 0.1]
sources:
  - work: the-egg-interactive-stories
    page: Interactive Stories, Overview
    quote: The choices made have a direct consequence on the course of the story and participants cannot usually reverse their decision.
deeper: The next level would hold option wording, preconditions, state changes, consequences and the rejoin policy.
---
