term: Barks
aka:
  - ambient dialogue
  - combat chatter
  - callouts
  - reaction lines
kind: concept
plain: Short context-triggered lines a character says about what it sees, does or feels.
does: Broadcasts hidden AI state and intentions, and gives companions personality with little dialogue.
ask: Which internal state change does the player never see, and which line would announce it?
cues:
  - A line fired when a character spots, hits, loses or misses something
  - A callout that reveals an intention or state
  - Companion chatter during travel, combat or exchange
trouble: Lines sound contrived, arrive out of context or fail to sell the situation.
