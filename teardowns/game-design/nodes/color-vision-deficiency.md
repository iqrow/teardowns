---
term: Colour-vision deficiency
aka: [colour-blind readability]
kind: concept
plain: Colour-vision deficiency changes which hue pairs are easy to distinguish, so an encoding must not assume one person's colour naming or one proof image represents every player.
does: Keeps essential information available when some colours merge, darken or lose their expected names.
ask: Which marks still communicate when hue differences are reduced or unavailable?
cues:
  - A red-green distinction has a second visible cue
  - Small symbols use enough size, brightness or shape to remain separate
  - A proof view exposes a collision that the ordinary view hides
range: Check confusable hue pairs and low-saturation or low-lightness marks, then confirm the result with people and the real display context.
trouble: A player cannot distinguish two roles, a dark colour vanishes, or a colourblind proof is treated as a universal prediction.
at: [0.62, 0.25, 0.06]
