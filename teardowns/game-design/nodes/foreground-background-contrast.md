---
term: Foreground/background contrast
aka: [can the mark stand off the field]
kind: knob
plain: Foreground/background contrast is the separation between a mark and the surface behind it, using brightness, saturation, outline or a backing panel.
does: Keeps text, objects and markers legible when the scene is busy or its colours shift.
ask: Can the player distinguish the object or text from its background without relying on hue?
cues:
  - Text sits on a field with a visibly different brightness
  - A thin marker gains an outline or backing plate against the scene
  - The same label remains legible over light and dark terrain
range: Bright-on-dark, dark-on-bright, or an outline and panel that maintain separation from the scene.
trouble: A marker disappears into the field, text becomes unreadable, or a hue pair has similar brightness.
at: [0.42, 0.48, 0.06]
sources:
  - work: masataka-okabe-and-kei-ito-color-universal-design
    page: How to select colors that are easier to distinguish
    quote: "there should be enough contrasts in brightness and saturation between texts/objects and backgrounds."
