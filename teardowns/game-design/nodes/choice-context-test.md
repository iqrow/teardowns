---
term: Decision variation test
aka: [does the answer change with the situation?]
kind: process
plain: Replay a decision across changed resources, threats, positions and goals, recording why the preferred action changes or stays the same. Then watch target players to distinguish a deliberate preference from misunderstanding.
does: Checks that repeated core-loop decisions stay sensitive to context rather than becoming rote.
ask: What reachable change in state makes a different action worth taking?
range: Use representative states with stated goals; compare alternatives and causal explanations, not a requirement that picks be evenly distributed.
trouble: The same answer survives every relevant change, or apparent variety comes only from players not knowing the rules.
