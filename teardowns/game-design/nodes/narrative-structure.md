---
term: Narrative structure
aka: [story architecture, branching structure, story flow]
kind: process
plain: Narrative structure arranges scenes, conversations, quests or storylets into the order and conditions in which players can encounter them.
does: Controls pacing, scope and how earlier actions shape later content.
ask: Which pieces are linear, branch, rejoin, float or repeat, and what state carries between them?
cues:
  - Scenes, conversations or quests in sequence
  - A branch, rejoin or repeat in the story
  - Earlier actions shaping later content
range: Linear outline, branching tree, branch-and-bottleneck, quest, open map, floating modules or loop-and-grow.
trouble: Branches explode beyond production capacity, reconvergence erases choices, or players cannot tell what state they are in.
at: [-0.9, 0.4, 0.1]
sources:
  - work: bruno-dias-introduction-a-discipline-if-we-can
    page: How stories are structured
    quote: Sometimes a game story can be written down as a linear outline; sometimes it's a branching tree structure; sometimes chunks of narrative are free-floating, and can be encountered in different orders.
deeper: The next level would hold story nodes, links, state variables, branch and rejoin rules, and authoring-scope checks.
