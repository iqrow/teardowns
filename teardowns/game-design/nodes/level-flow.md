aka: [movement flow, spatial flow]

does: Tunes movement so geometry supports the intended experience rather than merely connecting rooms.
ask: Does moving here feel safe or dangerous, simple or complex, fast or slow?

  - Movement along paths between areas
  - Fast or slow travel
  - Smooth or abrupt turns
range: Straight or curved, fast or slow, smooth or abrupt, safe or dangerous; verify it in blockout and playtest.
trouble: Players stall, overshoot, miss the intended turn, or find the route boring or confusing.
