---
term: Deviation test
aka: [breach payoff check, keep-or-break calculation]
kind: measure
plain: A deviation test compares the immediate benefit of breaking a commitment with the direct cost, automatic sanction and future payoff lost after the breach. Run it for every plausible state, not only the state in which the treaty was signed.
does: Finds promises that sound credible in negotiation but become irrational to keep when the trigger arrives.
ask: After the other side has relied on the deal, is keeping it still at least as good as breaking it under the rule's continuation?
range: Immediate gain; breach cost; future loss; continuation response
trouble: If the short-term gain exceeds every modeled consequence, the treaty is a request, not an enforceable commitment. [INFERENCE]
