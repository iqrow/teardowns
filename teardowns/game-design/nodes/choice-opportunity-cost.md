---
term: Opportunity cost
aka: [the alternative I cannot take]
kind: knob
plain: Opportunity cost is the valuable alternative forgone by committing time, resources or attention to an action. Tune the shared budget and each action's demands, not just its printed price.
does: Connects local decisions to the wider economy and action schedule.
ask: What else could this same time, money or attention have accomplished?
range: Set shared resource budget, action cost, occupied time and whether competing actions can overlap.
trouble: Everything can be done at once for free, or an apparent trade-off charges a resource that never runs short.
