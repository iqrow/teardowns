---
term: Unit role
aka: [unit job, unit archetype]
kind: concept
plain: A unit role is the job a unit performs—holding space, dealing damage, scouting, supporting, raiding or taking objectives—separate from the target type it counters.
does: Gives each roster entry a primary job and bounded secondary jobs before stats are tuned, so an anti-cavalry bonus does not become the unit's entire identity.
ask: What should this unit contribute when its preferred target is absent, unavailable or already answered?
range: Choose one primary job and at most one or two secondary jobs; record preferred targets, bad targets, required conditions and the failure state before tuning numbers.
trouble: If the only description is a target type such as anti-cavalry, the unit has a matchup bonus but no job; a generalist that owns too many jobs crowds out the roster.
