term: Outside option
aka: [alternative partner, fallback action]
kind: knob
plain: "An outside option is a credible alternative agreement available if a proposal is rejected; a unilateral fallback can be modeled as one. Keep it distinct from a disagreement or continuation payoff (what no deal yields) and from a threat (an action or claim meant to change expectations). [MODEL DISTINCTION]"
does: Separates an actionable alternative from the no-deal payoff and from a strategic threat, so the minimum acceptable agreement is not guessed.
ask: Which alternative can this player actually take, what no-deal payoff remains, and is any threat credible?
range: Alternative partner; unilateral alternative; disagreement payoff; strategic threat [separate]
trouble: If the alternative is not executable, the no-deal payoff is hidden, or the threat is not credible, proposals can stall without changing anyone's incentives. [INFERENCE]
