term: Pressure and countermeasure
aka: [the brake response]
kind: process
plain: A pressure raises a cost, threat or scarcity signal; a countermeasure supplies negative feedback that reduces its effect or changes the available choice.
does: Converts imbalance into a new decision instead of an automatic win.
ask: What response can the player recognise and choose?
cues:
  - Cost, threat or scarcity rising
  - A countermeasure reducing its effect
  - An available choice changing under pressure
