---
term: Deliberation time
aka: [how long I have to decide]
kind: knob
plain: Deliberation time is the interval in which the player can inspect and compare alternatives before a deadline or changing state forces commitment. Shortening it adds pressure without adding new rules.
does: Balances the reasoning burden against the intended tempo and player skill.
ask: Is failure caused by the intended time challenge, or by insufficient time to read the interface?
cues:
  - An interval before a deadline
  - A changing state before commitment
range: Set a decision deadline or threat speed; alternatively pause world time while choosing. Do not confuse real-world turn deadlines with in-world action duration.
trouble: Novices cannot read their choices before commitment, or unlimited thinking stalls a shared match.
