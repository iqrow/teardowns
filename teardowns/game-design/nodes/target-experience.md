---
term: Target experience
aka: [the feeling to test]
kind: concept
plain: A target experience is a concise statement of the situations, feelings or choices the player should have, used as a touchstone for playtesting.
does: Keeps iteration anchored to a human outcome rather than a list of features.
ask: What would we see a player do if the target were achieved?
