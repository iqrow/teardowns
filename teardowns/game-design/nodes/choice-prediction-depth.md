---
term: Prediction depth
aka: [how far ahead I must think]
kind: knob
plain: Prediction depth is how many linked effects the player must mentally simulate to compare actions. Tune the interaction chain and preview tools; more steps can create planning depth or simply exceed the available thinking time.
does: Controls the reasoning burden of a decision independently of the number of buttons.
ask: Can the player predict the important effect in the time allowed, and trace where a failed prediction went wrong?
range: Inspect immediate effect, next response and later resource or position effects; expose previews for the layers not meant to be the challenge.
trouble: Players understand every local rule but cannot follow their combined effect, or a preview makes all planning automatic.
