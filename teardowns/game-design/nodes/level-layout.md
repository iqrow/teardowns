aka: [top-down layout, floor plan, map sketch]
kind: process
plain: A layout is an initial visual plan of a level's structure, areas and key pacing beats.
does: Makes spatial relationships and intended flow discussable before expensive geometry and art harden decisions.
ask: What connects to what, and which route or beat should the plan make legible?
range: A napkin scribble can grow through parti thumbnails, bubble diagrams, a floor plan and gameplay markup.
trouble: The plan is polished but unplayable; players experience a maze or the team builds details that hide the core.
