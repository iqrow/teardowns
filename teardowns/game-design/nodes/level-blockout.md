---
term: Blockout
aka: [greybox, whitebox, blockmesh prototype]
kind: process
plain: A blockout is a low-detail playable version of a space made from simple shapes so movement, scale and encounters can be tested.
does: Exposes whether the geometry supports intended play before art and detail harden decisions.
ask: Can a player move, orient and solve the intended challenge in this rough space?
cues:
  - Simple shapes around a playable route
  - Movement through rough geometry
  - Encounters tested in the space
range: Paper or primitive boxes to engine greybox; iterate with playtests before an art pass.
trouble: The team polishes a route whose scale, sightlines or traversal fail once players move through it.
at: [0.62, -0.35, 0.08]
sources:
  - work: the-level-design-book-layout
    page: Layout, Why design a layout?
    quote: A layout drawing cannot tell you if your level works, only a blockout and playtest can begin answering that question.
deeper: The next level would hold movement metrics, primitives, traversal prototypes, encounter placeholders and blockout playtests.
