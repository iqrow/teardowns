---
term: Ludonarrative dissonance
aka: [story–mechanics mismatch, ludo-narrative conflict]
kind: concept
plain: Ludonarrative dissonance is a conflict between the story a game tells and the actions, incentives or values its mechanics require.
does: Flags when narrative promises and play teach incompatible meanings, weakening trust and emotional alignment.
ask: What is the player doing and being rewarded for, and does the fiction say that action means something else?
range: A small continuity mismatch to a systemic contradiction; passive (irrelevant rules) or active (conflicting rules).
trouble: Players call the protagonist out of character, treat the story as disposable, or optimize a behavior the fiction condemns.
at: [0.9, -0.65, 0.1]
sources:
  - work: gamedesign-gg-ludonarrative-dissonance
    page: Ludonarrative Dissonance
    quote: The clash that occurs when a game's mechanics express one thing about its world or protagonist while its story insists on another.
deeper: The next level would hold the narrative claim, mechanic incentive, player action, reward and a mismatch test.
