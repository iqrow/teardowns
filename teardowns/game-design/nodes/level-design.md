---
term: Level design
aka: [play-space design, map and level design]
kind: part
plain: Level design plans and builds the playable spaces where a game's rules become movement, navigation and encounters.
does: Turns mechanics and experience goals into spaces, routes, beats and readable affordances that shape what players do next.
ask: What should this space make players feel, notice and do, and how will the geometry support it?
cues:
  - Playable spaces with movement and navigation
  - Routes, beats and readable affordances
  - Encounters in a built space
range: From tightly scripted rooms and critical paths to open worlds, multiplayer lanes and player-made routes.
trouble: "The map feels like decoration or a maze: players miss goals, misread affordances, or fight the geometry instead of the intended challenge."
children: [level-experience-goal, level-layout, level-blockout, level-flow, level-critical-path, level-pacing, level-readability]
sources:

    page: What is level design
    quote: Level designers focus on shaping player behavior.
