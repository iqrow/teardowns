---
term: Narrative design
aka: [interactive narrative design, story systems design]
kind: part
plain: Narrative design structures a game's stories, how players learn them, and how they respond to player actions.
does: Aligns story structure, delivery channels and reactive state with player agency, game scope and the play experience.
ask: What story structure, delivery and response will make player action matter?
cues:
  - Stories learned through dialogue, text or sound
  - Player actions changing story response
range: Linear, branching, free-floating or systemic; text, dialogue, image, sound, space and rules.
trouble: Story arrives as disconnected exposition, choices promise change without consequences, or mechanics ask players to act against the stated fiction.
children: [narrative-structure, narrative-choice, narrative-state, narrative-worldbuilding, narrative-delivery, narrative-agency, ludonarrative-dissonance]
sources:
  - work: bruno-dias-introduction-a-discipline-if-we-can
    page: Definition of narrative design
    quote: Narrative design is the discipline that concerns itself with how stories are structured, how they become known to the player, and how they respond to the player's actions.
---
