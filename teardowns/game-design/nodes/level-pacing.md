---
term: Pacing
aka: [level rhythm, beat sequence]
kind: measure
plain: Pacing is the order and rhythm of activities and events the player experiences across a level.
does: Alternates intensity, information and rest so the player's attention and learning rise and recover at useful times.
ask: Why does this activity happen before that one, and when does the player get to breathe?
range: Beat sheet, flowchart or intensity graph; teach–test–twist; high/low intensity; authored, systemic or zone-based.
trouble: The level is a monotone slog, confusion arrives before teaching, or maximum intensity never lets players recover.
at: [-0.4, -0.65, 0.08]

deeper: The next level would hold beats, an intensity metric, information timing, rests, set pieces and variation.
