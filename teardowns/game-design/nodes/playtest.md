---
term: Playtest
aka: [put it in front of players]
kind: process
plain: A playtest puts a playable prototype or build in front of target players to see whether the intended experience is actually happening.
does: Supplies evidence about actual dynamics rather than imagined behaviour.
ask: What can players do without our explanation, and where do they diverge?
children: [playtest-session]
