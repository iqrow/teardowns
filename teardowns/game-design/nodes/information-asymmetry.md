---
term: Information asymmetry
aka: [private type, hidden intention, uneven knowledge]
kind: concept
plain: Information asymmetry exists when players do not share the same relevant facts, types, plans or observations. The receiver must act on a belief about what the sender knows or intends, while the sender may benefit from revealing, concealing or distorting it.
does: Creates room for inference, bluffing, screening and mistaken coordination instead of making every choice a calculation from a common state.
ask: Which player knows something the others do not, and how can that private fact change the next action?
trouble: If hidden information cannot be learned, inferred or acted on, it adds confusion rather than a decision. [INFERENCE]
