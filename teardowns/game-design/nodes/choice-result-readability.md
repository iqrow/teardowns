term: Discernible outcome
aka: [I can tell what my action did]
kind: measure
plain: An outcome is discernible when the player can identify the relevant change and connect it to the action. Observe whether the player can explain the result, not merely whether a flashy effect played.
does: Lets the player revise a mental model and make a better-informed next choice.
ask: Can a target player tell what changed, which action caused it and whether it helped?
cues:
  - A relevant change after an action
  - Health changing with the action
  - The result tied to its cause
range: Record correct causal explanations, missed changes and time until recognition; compare channels and feedback delay.
trouble: A health change is buried in noise, delayed feedback is attributed to the wrong action, or a celebration hides a bad tactical result.
