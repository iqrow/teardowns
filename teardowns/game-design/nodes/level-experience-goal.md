---
term: Experience goal
aka: [player experience goal, design goal]
kind: knob
plain: An experience goal states what the player should learn, feel or do in a level.
does: Gives layout, pacing and encounter choices a target instead of letting geometry dictate the experience.
ask: What should the player learn, feel, or do here?
range: Specific teaching or feeling goals are easier to design than broad aspirations; a goal may be concrete or abstract.
trouble: A level has a pile of mechanics or rooms but no shared purpose, so playtest observations cannot tell which outcome is intended.
at: [-0.62, 0.35, 0.08]
