---
term: Agency contract
aka: [player agency, promise of consequence]
kind: knob
plain: An agency contract is the set of expectations about which player actions can shape the story and how visibly they matter.
does: Sets the boundary between authored focus and player freedom so choices feel intentional rather than arbitrary.
ask: What kind of control does the game promise, and where will it honor or deliberately deny that promise?
cues:
  - Player action changing story state
  - An outcome visibly shaped by a choice
range: No story response to local variation to persistent world-state changes; responses may be explicit, clued or hidden.
trouble: The game promises freedom but funnels every action, or changes outcomes without giving the player a readable cause.
at: [-0.2, -0.65, 0.1]
sources:
  - work: bruno-dias-introduction-a-discipline-if-we-can
    page: How stories respond to the player's actions
    quote: Often, we're somewhere between those two extremes, trying to balance questions of player agency against the needs of experiential focus and game scope.
deeper: The next level would hold the promise, action scope, response delay, feedback, exceptions and player expectations.
