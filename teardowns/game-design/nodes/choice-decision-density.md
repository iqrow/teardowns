---
term: Meaningful decision density
aka: [choices per stretch of play]
kind: measure
plain: Decision density records how often play presents a fresh comparison between worthwhile alternatives, not how many inputs the player performs. Mark decision timestamps and repeated solved situations in a play trace.
does: Reveals empty stretches and overload while separating tactical choice from mechanical execution.
ask: Where does a new dilemma occur, and where is the player merely repeating an already settled answer?
range: Measure intervals between marked decisions; annotate option set, relevant state change and player familiarity. No universal target rate.
trouble: Rapid clicking disguises a long decision void, or important dilemmas arrive before the previous result can be understood.
