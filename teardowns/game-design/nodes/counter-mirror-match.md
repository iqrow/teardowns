term: Mirror match
aka: [same-unit matchup, mirror]
kind: concept
plain: A mirror match pits the same unit, role or roster against itself, removing the intended composition edge so the designer can see what execution, timing, positioning and information contribute.
does: Checks that a unit remains interesting when its counter relationship disappears and reveals first-move, map or control advantages that can hide inside the matrix.
ask: When both sides choose the same job, what decides the exchange, and is that decision legible and recoverable?
range: Use equal resources, unit count, map, upgrades and information where possible; separately record first move, initiative, terrain and control assumptions.
trouble: A mirror decided by an untelegraphed first move, spawn or hidden roster advantage is not measuring only skill; a perfectly static mirror may expose no meaningful decision.
