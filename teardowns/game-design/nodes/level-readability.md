---
term: Readability
aka: [spatial legibility, navigational readability]
kind: concept
plain: Readability is whether players can understand the space, its objects and where they can or should go.
does: Lets art, materials, landmarks and affordances communicate spatial logic without extra explanation.
ask: Can players tell what matters, what is usable, and where they can go?
range: Clear to noisy, from abstract blockout to art-passed scene; consistency matters more than decoration.
trouble: Visual noise hides routes or interactables; players get lost, misread a door, or cannot distinguish gameplay from set dressing.
at: [0.75, 0.55, 0.08]
sources:
  - work: the-level-design-book-environment-art
    page: Environment Art, Readability
    quote: Readability is about whether the player can walk around your level and read what they are looking at.
deeper: The next level would hold landmarks, signposting, visual language, contrast, affordances and wayfinding tests.
