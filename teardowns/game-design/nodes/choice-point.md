---
term: Choice point
aka: [the decision]
kind: concept
plain: A choice point is a state in which two or more actions have different consequences worth weighing.
does: Creates agency and makes the core loop more than a sequence of button presses.
ask: What can the player choose, and what trade-off does each option carry?
trouble: Two buttons lead to the same useful result, one option wins in every relevant state, or the player cannot explain what choosing differently would change.
