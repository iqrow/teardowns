term: Free riding
aka: [benefit without contribution, public-good dilemma]
kind: measure
plain: Free riding is the gap between receiving a shared benefit and paying the contribution that produces it. Measure each player's marginal contribution, private payoff, shared payoff and the threshold at which noncontributors can no longer be carried.
does: Reveals when rational local choices can underprovide a useful collective action.
ask: Can this player receive the benefit if they contribute nothing, and what selective incentive changes that calculation?
range: Contribution rate; beneficiary-to-contributor ratio; provision threshold; sanction or reward
