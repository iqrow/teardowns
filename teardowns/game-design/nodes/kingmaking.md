---
term: Kingmaking
aka: [spoiler power, choosing another winner]
kind: concept
plain: Kingmaking occurs when a player can no longer secure their own victory but still has an action that decides which rival wins. The action may follow score preference, reciprocity, spite, roleplay or a remaining best move; the rule should not assume one motive.
does: Makes the endgame payoff for a losing player and the resulting winner-selection power visible.
ask: When a player loses their own path, what can they still change, and what payoff tells them which change to choose?
trouble: If the player must continue but every legal move only chooses another winner, agency can feel detached from personal goals; if that power is intended, make its motive and consequence legible. [INFERENCE]
