---
term: Perceptual separation
aka: [differences that survive perception]
kind: concept
plain: "Perceptual separation is the visible distance between marks as people actually see them; it can come from lightness, chroma, hue, texture or shape rather than equal steps around a hue wheel."
does: Separates similar marks in the intended viewing conditions; grayscale and colour-vision-deficient views each need their own check.
ask: Which pair is hardest to tell apart at the size players see?
cues:
  - Swatches remain distinct when the display is desaturated
  - A lightness step separates two similar hues
  - Small markers keep different silhouettes instead of relying on hue alone
range: Hue-only spacing, lightness changes, or combined hue, chroma, lightness, texture and shape differences.
trouble: Equal hue spacing leaves some pairs visibly closer than others, or a palette-editor distinction disappears in play.
at: [0.05, 0.48, 0.06]
