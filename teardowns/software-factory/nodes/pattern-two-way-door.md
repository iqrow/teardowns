---
term: Two-way door
aka:
  - reversible decision
  - one-way door
  - Type 1 and Type 2 decisions
kind: pattern
plain: Sort decisions by how hard they are to undo. Make the reversible ones quickly and close to the work. Slow down, and bring in more people, only for the few that are costly or impossible to reverse.
ask: If this turns out to be wrong, how costly is it to undo?
sources:

  - work: amazon-2015-shareholder-letter

    quote: "But most decisions aren’t like that … they are changeable, reversible … they’re two-way doors."
