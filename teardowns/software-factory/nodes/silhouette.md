---
term: Silhouette
aka:
  - silhouette test
  - silhouette readability
kind: concept
plain: "The outer contour a character presents when reduced to a solid black shape. It should stay recognisable at the smallest useful display size, with broad proportions and shape breaks doing the work instead of internal detail."
does: Lets people tell characters apart and read a pose when shading, colour and fine detail disappear.
ask: If each character were a solid-black thumbnail, could you tell them apart and read its pose?
cues:
  - A solid-black thumbnail still identifies the character
  - Broad proportions and major appendages make different contours
  - The pose reads without facial marks, colour or internal detail
  - Characters keep separate outer contours at the smallest useful size
trouble: "Characters collapse into the same outline, a pose hides their distinguishing masses, or recognition depends on facial marks, colour or tiny accessories."
