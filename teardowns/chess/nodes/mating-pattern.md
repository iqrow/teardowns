term: Mating pattern
aka:
  - checkmate pattern
  - mating net
kind: concept
plain: A mating pattern is a recurring arrangement that leaves the checked king with no legal escape, capture, or interposition. Typical patterns include back-rank mates, smothered mates, corridor mates, and the basic king-and-rook or king-and-queen finishes.
does: Gives a player a concrete picture of the final mating net and the defensive squares that must be removed or covered.
ask: Which escape squares, checking piece, and support piece make the mate legal?
cues:
  - Checked king with no escape
  - Checking piece and support piece
  - Back-rank, smothered or corridor arrangement
trouble: The attacker sees a familiar picture but allows stalemate, an interposition, or a king capture.
