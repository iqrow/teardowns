---
term: Time trouble
aka:
  - time pressure
  - zeitnot
kind: concept
plain: Time trouble is pressure created when the remaining clock is too small for comfortable decisions, so calculation and execution must be compressed before the flag falls.
does: Turns the clock into an immediate tactical constraint and can decide a game even when the board position is playable.
ask: What is the simplest legal, safe move before the flag falls?
cues:
  - Small remaining clock
  - Flag close while the position remains playable
at: [0.28, -0.18, 0.04]
