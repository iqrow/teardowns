plain: A pattern catalogue is an indexed collection of named positions with a diagram, trigger conditions, typical continuation, defensive refutation, and links to related patterns. Its useful unit is a reusable configuration, not a raw count of positions.
does: Makes a large body of recurring chess knowledge searchable by subject term and comparable across openings, middlegames, tactics, and endings.

range: Tactical, positional, opening, endgame, mating, and piece-configuration families; expand by subject terms rather than by arbitrary enumeration.
trouble: The index becomes a list of labels or puzzles with no position, conditions, explanation, or retrieval test.
