term: Candidate moves
aka:
  - shortlist
  - serious candidates
kind: process
plain: Candidate moves are the small shortlist worth calculating in a critical position. Start with forcing moves, then add a necessary defence or a strong positional move; compare the resulting positions instead of choosing from the whole legal move list.
does: Makes calculation finite and prevents an attractive first idea from becoming an untested decision.
ask: Which two or three moves deserve a concrete line, and what opponent reply tests each one?
range: Usually a short list; the position decides whether defence or a quiet move belongs beside forcing moves.
trouble: A player calculates one favourite move, or generates so many moves that no line is checked fully.
