term: Puzzle training
aka:
  - tactical exercises
  - solving drills
kind: process
plain: Puzzle training presents a position, asks for the best continuation, and then requires an explanation of the motif and the failed alternatives. Mix fast recognition with slower calculation, and revisit misses until the pattern is retrievable in a changed position.
does: Builds a testable bridge from named patterns to concrete move selection and defensive awareness.
ask: What pattern did the position test, why did the solution work, and what changed in the failed attempt?
cues:
  - Position shown
  - Best continuation requested
  - Motif explanation
range: Basic motifs first, then more complex combinations, positional and endgame patterns, with recurring review of misses.
trouble: Guessing the first move, memorising a line without the position, or measuring only speed and streaks.
