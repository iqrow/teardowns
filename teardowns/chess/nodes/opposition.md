---
term: Opposition
aka:
  - king opposition
  - direct opposition
kind: concept
plain: Opposition is the king-to-king geometry in which the kings face one another with an odd number of squares between them; direct opposition has one square. Because a player must move, the side that can hand the move back or force the other king away may reach the key square.
does: Controls king entry and tempo in pawn endings and other reduced-material positions.
ask: Who has the move, which king can take the opposition, and what happens after the forced king step?
cues:
  - Kings facing one another
  - Odd number of squares between the kings
trouble: The player remembers “opposition” without counting squares, accounting for diagonal or distant forms, or checking pawn tempos.
