---
term: Colour grade
aka:
  - color correction
  - grading
  - LUT
kind: process
plain: First correction (fix exposure and white balance so shots match), then
  grading (a chosen look). Scopes such as the waveform and vectorscope measure
  it.
does: Makes shots match and skin look natural; sets the mood.
ask: Do skin tones look right, and do the shots match?
cues:
  - "Shots matching in exposure and white balance"
  - "Natural-looking skin tones"
  - "Shadows or skin jumping orange or green"
trouble: Orange or green skin, crushed shadows, or shots that jump in colour.
