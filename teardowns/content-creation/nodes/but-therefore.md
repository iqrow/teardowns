term: But / therefore
aka:
  - and then problem
  - causality
kind: concept
plain: "Trey Parker and Matt Stone’s rule: link beats with “but” or “therefore”,
  never “and then”. “Therefore” gives cause; “but” gives complication."
does: Turns a list of points into a chain the viewer has to follow.
ask: Between these two beats, is it “but”, “therefore” or just “and then”?
trouble: The script reads as a list; any section could be swapped with any other.
