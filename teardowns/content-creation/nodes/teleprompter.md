term: Teleprompter
aka:
  - prompter
  - autocue
kind: part
plain: A screen under a sheet of angled beam-splitter glass in front of the
  lens. The glass reflects the mirrored script toward you, and the camera films
  through it. A common glass passes about 70% of the light and reflects about
  30%.
does: Lets you read the script while looking into the lens.
ask: Is the text speed set to my natural pace, and are my eyes moving visibly?
trouble: Eyes visibly reading left to right, or a flat, read-aloud voice.
