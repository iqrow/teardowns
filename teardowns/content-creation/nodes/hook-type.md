term: Hook type
aka:
  - opening line
  - kind of hook
kind: knob
plain: "Interchangeable ways to open: a question, a bold claim, the result
  first, the stakes, the middle of the action, or a striking image."
does: Picks the fastest route from the package’s promise to the viewer’s reason
  to stay.
ask: Which opening matches this package and this viewer?
cues:
  - "A question, bold claim or result first"
  - "Stakes or challenge in the opening"
  - "Middle of the action or a striking image"
trouble: Every video opens the same way, or the hook promises something the
  video does not deliver.
