  demo, a detail. The main shot of you talking is the A-roll."
does: Shows instead of tells, and hides cuts in the talking shot.
ask: What could the viewer see here instead of my face?
cues:
  - "A demo or detail over the main shot"
  - "A talking-head cut hidden by related footage"
trouble: A wall of talking head, or B-roll that has nothing to do with the words.
