---
term: Animatic
aka:
  - storyboard timed to the voice-over
  - boardomatic
  - timing pass
kind: process
plain: An animatic is a storyboard edited into a rough video, with each panel timed and optional voice, music, sound effects, camera moves and transitions.
does: Tests the order, meaning, pace and audio sync while changes are still cheap, before finished animation is built.
ask: Does each graphic arrive on the word, beat or action it illustrates?
cues:
  - "Storyboard panels in sequence on an editing timeline"
  - "Temporary voice, music or sound effects under the panels"
  - "Timecode, camera moves or transitions marking the intended timing"
trouble: Panels are attractive but the sequence drags, the sound lands away from the action, or a graphic arrives before the viewer knows why it is there.
range: "Rough panels, sound-timed panels, or a camera and transition pass."

variants:
  - label: Timing pass
    items:
      - id: animatic-rough
        name: Rough animatic
        plain: A rough storyboard sequence, sometimes with a soundtrack, used to preview what a sequence will look like.
        why: Its defining feature is a rough preview of a sequence, not polished artwork or final animation.
        sources:
          - work: adobe-animatics
            page: What is the definition of animatics?
            quote: "An animatic is an animated storyboard. It’s a rough draught of a sequence or film, sometimes with a soundtrack, used to help filmmakers get an idea of what a sequence will look like."
      - id: animatic-sound-timed
        name: Sound-timed animatic
        plain: A panel sequence timed around temporary dialogue, music or effects.
        why: It is sound-timed when audio sets the action's pace, whatever the audio's final status or genre.
        sources:
          - work: toon-boom-animatic
            page: How to Create an Animatic
            quote: "Sound effects, voice clips and music tracks, be them provisional or final, can be added so that action can be paced around them"
      - id: animatic-camera
        name: Camera and transition animatic
        plain: A timed board that also tests camera movement, framing and scene transitions.
        why: The item is defined by testing screen movement and scene changes before production, not by using a particular storyboard application.
        sources:
          - work: toon-boom-animatic
            page: How to Create an Animatic
            quote: "It allows you to visually adjust the timing of each panel, to import, cue and clip sound and video clips, to animate camera movements, to animate layers and to add transitions between scenes."
