  - posting schedule

kind: knob
plain: How often and when you publish. YouTube found no link between the gap
  between uploads and view growth, and says daily or weekly uploads are not
  required. A pace you can keep matters more.
does: Sets how much practice and how many chances you get, against how much time
  each video gets.
ask: What pace can I keep for a year without the videos getting worse?
trouble: Bursts of uploads and long gaps, or quality falling to hit a schedule.
