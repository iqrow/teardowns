term: Foil
aka:
  - counterpoint
  - counter-argument
  - opposing view
  - the sceptic
  - steelman
kind: concept
plain: A person, view or thing set beside your subject so the contrast makes it
  clearer. In an argued video it is the strongest case against your point,
  stated fairly, then answered.
does: Helps the viewer understand the subject by contrast, while the subject
  stays the star.
ask: Would someone who holds this view say I stated it fairly?
cues:
  - "The opposing source on screen before the reply"
  - "Two characters or sides shown together, one making the other stand out"
trouble: "A straw man: the opposing view is stated weakly, so answering it does
  not answer the real argument. Or the foil takes over, and viewers learn more
  about it than about the subject."
