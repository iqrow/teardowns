---
term: Hook stack
aka:
  - hook stacking
  - layered hook
  - spoken, text and visual hook
  - lock-in zone
kind: knob
plain: "What the opening piles up so the right viewer stays. Creators use the
  name for two stacks: the layers the viewer sees, reads and hears in the first
  two or three seconds, plus a follow-up line; or the parts packed into the
  first sentence itself: who it is for, the result, a number, what they get
  and the pain it avoids."
does: Qualifies the viewer and makes the promise concrete before they can
  scroll, then holds them past the first line.
ask: Does the first line say who it is for and what they get, and do the
  image, the on-screen text and the voice say the same thing?
cues:
  - "Large on-screen text in the first frame, apart from the captions"
  - "A first spoken line about what the image shows"
  - "A second line that adds proof, a contrast or an unexplained name"
trouble: The opening never moves beyond the hook, so it fails to hold attention
  and the viewer skips.
