---
term: Title and thumbnail A/B test
aka:
  - Test & Compare
  - thumbnail testing
  - A/B testing
kind: process
plain: YouTube shows up to three titles, thumbnails or both to different viewers
  at once, then picks the one with the most watch time, not the most clicks.
does: Replaces arguing about the package with evidence from real viewers.
ask: Which version earns the most watch time, not just the most clicks?
cues:
  - "Different titles or thumbnails shown at once"
  - "A watch-time comparison between versions"
range: Up to 3 variants; a few days to 2 weeks; not available for Shorts (YouTube)
trouble: You test three near-identical images and learn nothing.
at:
  - 0.5727
  - 0.4136
  - 0.012
pattern: pattern-build-test-learn
sources:
  - work: youtube-help-a-b-test-titles-thumbnails
    page: Test setup
    quote: Upload up to 3 thumbnails and/or titles to test.
  - work: youtube-help-a-b-test-titles-thumbnails
    page: Watch-time winner
    quote: At the end of the test, the title or combination of title and thumbnail with the highest watch time will be shown to all viewers.
