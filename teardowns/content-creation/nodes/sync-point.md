does: "Makes an added sound feel like part of the picture."
ask: "Which part of this sound lands on which frame?"
cues:
  - "A hit landing with the title, not after it"
  - "A riser that stops on the cut"
range: "Frame-accurate: zoom the timeline in until you can place the sound on the exact frame."
trouble: "A sound two or three frames off the cut, which looks and sounds sloppy."

sources:

    page: "Core terms: Sync"
    quote: "Sync. Aligning a sound to the exact frame of the action it belongs to, so a slam lands with the door and a footstep lands with the foot. Good sync is what makes an added effect feel like it was captured on set."
  - work: "nick-metcalfe-sound-design-for-visual-media"
