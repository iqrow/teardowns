term: Open loop
aka:
  - question to the end
  - teaser
  - dramatic question
kind: concept
plain: A specific question raised now and answered later. Close small loops as
  you go and keep one big one for the end.
does: Gives the viewer a reason to watch the next section.
ask: Which question is open right now, and when do I close it?
cues:
  - "Small answers closing while one large question remains"
  - "A later section resolving the earlier question"
trouble: Nothing is open in the middle, so viewers leave; or every answer is
  withheld and viewers feel strung along.
