term: Click-through rate
aka:
  - CTR
  - impressions CTR
kind: measure
plain: The share of impressions that became views. YouTube says half of all
  channels and videos have an impressions CTR between 2% and 10%.
does: Scores the packaging against everything else on the viewer’s screen.
ask: Is CTR low everywhere, or only in one traffic source?
cues:
  - "Impressions and views compared as a share"
  - "Low browse CTR beside fine search CTR"
range: 2–10% for half of channels (YouTube); falls as a video reaches broader
  audiences
trouble: "Low CTR from browse features while search CTR is fine: the package
  does not sell to casual viewers."
