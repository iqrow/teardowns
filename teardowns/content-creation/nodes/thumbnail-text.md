---
term: Thumbnail text
aka:
  - words on the thumbnail
  - text overlay
kind: knob
plain: The few words painted on the image. Galloway usually uses two or three
  and rarely more than five or six; some channels use none.
does: Adds what the image cannot say, and works with the title instead of
  repeating it.
ask: Would the thumbnail lose anything if I deleted these words?
cues:
  - "Words adding what the image cannot say"
  - "Text readable at feed size"
range: 0–3 words typical; ≤ 5–6 (Galloway)
trouble: A sentence on the thumbnail that nobody can read at feed size.
at:
  - 0.3563
  - -0.2578
  - 0.012

  - work: youtube-help-thumbnail-title-tips
    page: Readable text and complexity
