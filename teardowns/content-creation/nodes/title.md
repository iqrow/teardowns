term: Title
aka:
  - video title
  - headline
kind: part
plain: The words beside the thumbnail. YouTube allows 100 characters but may
  show only the start, so the important words go first.
does: Tells the viewer what they get, and carries the search words when people
  search.
ask: Does it add to the thumbnail rather than repeat it?
cues:
  - "Important words visible at the start"
  - "Title and thumbnail adding rather than repeating"
range: Max 100 characters (YouTube); Galloway’s strongest titles are often about
  50 or fewer
trouble: The key word is cut off on phones, or title and thumbnail say the same
  thing twice.
