plain: "Music under the voice, turned down (ducked) whenever someone speaks.
  Every track needs a licence that covers your use: music in YouTube’s Shorts
  tools has its own limits, and other music can bring a Content ID claim.
  Choose the music's storytelling purpose before its genre. Compare pacing,
  timbre, instrumentation and emotional fit while playing it against the voice.
  Include no music as a candidate; a bed need not fill every pause."
