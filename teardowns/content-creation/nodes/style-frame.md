---
term: Style frames
aka:
  - design boards
  - style boards
  - look development
  - mood board
kind: process
plain: Style frames are finished still images that convey the visual mood or feeling of a piece.
does: Gives a concept a visual form.
ask: Do these frames show the visual rules clearly enough for another person to animate them?
cues:
  - "A finished still with its type, colour, composition and surface treatment visible"
  - "Several frames sharing one visual language while showing different moments"
  - "Swatches, type samples, reference images or notes beside the proposed frame"
trouble: A frame is attractive but says nothing about motion, the frames disagree on visual rules, or the board hides details another animator needs.
range: "Single style frame, connected design board or storyboard design board."
variants:
  - label: Board
    items:
      - id: style-single-frame
        name: Single style frame
        plain: A style frame conveys the visual mood or feeling of a piece.
        why: It is a style frame because it conveys a piece's visual treatment, not because it is a mood image or a finished animation.
        sources:

            quote: "Style frames exemplify the image-making aspects of design for motion by conveying the mood or feeling of a piece, whereas design boards represent storytelling by demonstrating a sequence of events in chronological order."
      - id: style-design-board
        name: Design board
        plain: A design board represents storytelling through a chronological sequence of events.
        why: A design board is a sequence of visual storytelling decisions, not a random collection of references or a finished timeline.
        sources:
          - work: austin-shaw-design-for-motion

      - id: style-storyboard-board
        name: Storyboard design board
        plain: A board works out the visual elements that best suit a story.
        why: It is story information when the board develops visual elements for a story, not simply because it contains drawings.
        sources:
