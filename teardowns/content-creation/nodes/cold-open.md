---
term: Cold open
aka:
  - start in the action
  - teaser
  - no intro
kind: process
plain: Starting with a strong moment from inside the story, before any setup,
  logo or greeting. MrBeast’s guide says the first minute loses the most
  viewers, so plan it hardest.
does: Gives the viewer the reason to stay before you ask them for attention.
ask: What is the most interesting moment, and can it come first?
cues:
  - "No logo or greeting before the moment"
  - "The story already in action at the opening"
trouble: The video opens with “Hey guys, welcome back”, a logo or the backstory.
