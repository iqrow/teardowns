term: Motion graphics
aka:
  - text cards
  - graphics
  - zooms
  - mograph
  - motion design
  - animated graphics
kind: part
plain: "Motion graphics are graphics with movement: text and design elements set in motion."
does: Turns a static design into a short piece of animation that entertains or informs.
ask: What is moving, why does it move, and what motion pattern would make this maker recognisable?
cues:
  - "Text, shapes or logos entering, changing or leaving over footage"
  - "Animated charts, callouts or diagrams that expose structure"
  - "A repeated type, colour and movement vocabulary across videos"
trouble: Decorative movement obscures the message, default presets erase visual voice, or overlays leave too little time to read.

variants:
  - label: Style
    items:
      - id: style-flat-2d-vector
        name: Flat 2D vector
        plain: Vector graphic elements built from paths, fills and strokes.
        why: The shared feature is vector graphics built from paths, fills and strokes, not a particular subject or platform.
        sources:
          - work: adobe-after-effects-shape-layers-vector
            page: About shapes and shape layers
            quote: "Shape layers contain vector graphics objects called shapes. By default, a shape consists of a path, a stroke, and a fill."
      - id: style-kinetic-type
        name: Kinetic type
        plain: Letterforms and words are animated as visual elements over time.
        why: Kinetic type is defined by animating properties of individual characters or a range, not by a particular font, colour or spoken subject.
        sources:
          - work: adobe-after-effects-animating-text
            page: Animating text
            quote: "Use text animators and selectors to animate many properties of individual characters or a range of characters."
      - id: style-3d
        name: 3D
        plain: A 3D scene uses objects, lights and cameras.
        why: The defining feature is a 3D scene containing spatial objects, lights and cameras, whatever the brand, colour or message.
        sources:
          - work: blackmagic-fusion-motion-graphics
            page: True 3D Workspace
            quote: "The 3D merge tool lets you add an unlimited number of objects, lights and cameras into your scene."
      - id: style-cel-frame
        name: Cel or frame-by-frame
        plain: Graphic movement switches between held poses rather than interpolating between them.
        why: The defining feature is an abrupt change from one held pose to the next, not whether the drawing looks flat, rough or polished.
        sources:
          - work: adobe-after-effects-keyframe-interpolation
            page: Hold interpolation
            quote: "Hold interpolation is available only as a temporal interpolation method. Use it to change the value of a layer property over time, but without a gradual transition."
      - id: style-collage-mixed-media
        name: Collage or mixed media
        plain: Photographs and texture from another layer combine into one collage.
        why: The item is defined by combining visual materials, not by the topic, platform or one particular texture.
        sources:
          - work: adobe-after-effects-animate-picture
            page: Animate photos with a parallax effect
            quote: "The goal is to isolate objects in the foreground and provide sufficient elements — from one or more photographs — to create a convincing background."
          - work: adobe-after-effects-stylize-effects
            page: Texturize effect
            quote: "The Texturize effect gives a layer the appearance of having the texture of another layer."
      - id: style-screen-capture-callouts
        name: Screen capture with callouts
        plain: A screen recording annotated with callouts and cursor effects, breaking a complex process into simple steps.
        why: The defining feature is animated annotation used to explain a process, not the software or subject being demonstrated.
        sources:
          - work: adobe-motion-graphics-definition
            page: The advertising and social media age
            quote: "Creative agencies began to employ talented designers to create short, animated (and often annotated) graphics to advertise their client’s products and services — often to break down complex processes into simple steps."
