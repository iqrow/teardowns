---
term: Jump cut
aka:
  - cutting out pauses
  - cutting the ums
kind: process
plain: Cuts inside one continuous shot that remove pauses, mistakes and filler,
  so the subject seems to jump slightly. Standard for talking-head video.
does: Compresses time and removes dead air.
ask: Is this pause doing work, or is it dead air?
cues:
  - "A slight jump in the subject"
  - "Speech that feels breathless when every breath is cut"
trouble: Every breath gone, so the speech feels breathless and robotic.
