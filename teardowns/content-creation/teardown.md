A video post wins twice: first the click or the scroll-stop, then the minutes that follow. Creators plan the idea and its packaging, the title and thumbnail, before they film, because those decide whether anyone taps. The hook, story, picture, sound and edit then decide whether viewers stay to the payoff. The platform’s recommendation system amplifies whatever viewers respond to, so analytics such as click-through rate and the retention graph show which part failed. The work is a cycle: make, publish, review, and carry the lesson into the next video.
