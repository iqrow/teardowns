---
term: Hero asset
aka:
  - landmark prop
  - hero building
  - focal asset
kind: part
plain: A unique structure or prop that gives an important place a memorable shape. It earns extra modelling, material and lighting attention because it carries orientation or identity.
does: Anchors a place and gives the player a visual reference for where they are.
ask: "Why is this piece unique, where will it be seen from, and what gameplay or story role does it serve?"
at: [0.48, 0.52, 0.04]
cues:
  - One silhouette is visibly distinct from nearby repeated forms
  - The asset has a clear approach, view or contrast around it
  - Its base remains open enough to read nearby gameplay space
sources:
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book
    page: Environment Art
    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/env-art
    quote: "hero prop / hero building should ground an important place in the level"
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book
    page: Composition
    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/massing/composition
    quote: "A landmark or point of interest (POI) is a unique and memorable shape, mass, or location in a level."
