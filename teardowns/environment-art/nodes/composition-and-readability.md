---
term: Composition and readability
aka:
  - visual hierarchy
  - scene read
  - clarity pass
kind: concept
plain: The organisation of masses, values, detail and playable shapes so the player can understand the space at a glance and over time. Composition makes some parts important, while readability keeps routes, affordances and threats distinct.
does: Makes the world legible, directs attention by useful contrast and preserves play as detail accumulates.
ask: What should the player notice first, what can wait, and can the playable layer still be read when the scene is busy?

cues:
  - Large light and dark groups remain distinct in the full scene
  - Important shapes have clear silhouettes and breathing room
  - Routes, cover, interactables and threats separate from decoration
