---
term: Focal point
aka:
  - point of interest
  - landmark read
  - visual anchor
kind: concept
plain: A place or form that attracts attention through meaningful contrast, framing, position or function. It may be a landmark, an interaction space or the next area the player needs to understand.
does: Gives the scene a readable priority and helps the player orient within it.
ask: What should be noticed first from the intended approach, and what contrast makes that useful?
at: [0.65, -0.18, 0.04]
cues:
  - A distinct silhouette or value contrast anchors the view
  - A path, frame or open sightline presents the important form
  - Supporting detail becomes quieter around the anchor
sources:
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book
    page: Composition
    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/massing/composition
    quote: "At the top of the hierarchy are key landmarks, focal points, and central areas that contrast meaningfully with their surroundings and anchor the rest of the composition."
