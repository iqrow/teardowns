---
term: Art direction
aka:
  - look development
  - visual direction
kind: process
plain: The process of deciding a world's theme, gameplay emphasis, style, palette and shapes before an art pass. It turns a broad idea into choices artists can check.
does: Sets the visual constraints for an art pass.
ask: What must every asset share, and which references make that rule concrete?
children:
  - reference
  - visual-pillars
  - color-palette
  - shape-language
  - cohesion
cues:
  - A reference board groups photos, paintings and game stills
  - Short visual rules sit beside palette and shape studies
  - Asset examples repeat the same colour and silhouette decisions
sources:
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book
    page: Environment Art
    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/env-art
    quote: "Before you begin an art pass, you should to be able to answer these questions: Theme. What is the time of day, climate, and location for the level? Gameplay. Which parts need emphasis? What should be simple, what should be detailed? Style. Realistic or stylized? What type of realism, what type of style? Palette. What's the overall color palette, what's the mood? What kinds of shapes?"
