---
term: Environmental storytelling
aka:
  - story in the space
  - implied history
  - visual narrative
kind: concept
plain: The arrangement of rooms, damage, props and traces that conveys or implies a past event without a separate narration. It works through what the player notices while using the space.
does: Makes the world carry history and consequence through its physical state.
ask: What happened here, and which physical traces let the player infer it?
at: [-0.38, 0.82, -0.24]
cues:
  - A broken route, moved object or abandoned tool implies an event
  - Prop relationships suggest who used a space and what they did
  - Damage, stains or repairs form a readable sequence through the area
sources:
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book

    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/env-art/storytelling
    quote: "In level design, environmental storytelling is about building a level to convey or imply a past event."
