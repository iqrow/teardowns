term: Set dressing
aka:
  - clutter pass
  - detail props
  - dressing pass
kind: process
plain: "Set dressing places secondary props, foliage, debris and small variations in clusters around the main forms."
does: "Adds detail that helps players parse the space without competing with important forms."
ask: Which small objects make this place believable, and which cluster or gap keeps the read clear?
at: [0.1, 0.25, 0.05]
pattern: pattern-breakup
cues:
  - Small props repeat in loose clusters rather than a regular grid
  - Empty areas remain around important silhouettes and routes
  - Debris, plants and furniture gather near plausible use or edges
sources:
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book
    page: Environment Art
    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/env-art
    quote: "To help players parse and understand the game world more easily, try to compose the set dressing in clusters of related details."
