---
term: Modular kit
aka:
  - modular environment
  - tileset
  - snap kit
  - trim sheet
kind: part
plain: A set of meshes and material parts made to snap together into many structures. A good kit has clear footprints, useful connectors and enough variation to avoid a repeated stamp.
does: Builds a wide range of spaces from fewer tested pieces.
ask: What grid, footprint and connectors let these pieces build the required spaces without visible seams?
at: [-0.62, 0.38, 0.04]
pattern: pattern-breakup
cues:
  - Walls, floors, corners and openings meet on a repeatable grid
  - Trim bands and seams continue across several modules
  - A kit can make both a straight run and a corner without a gap
trouble: "Gaps, paper thin floors, visible seams, or a repeated stamp that gives the whole scene away."
sources:
  - work: robert-yang-the-level-design-book
    page: Environment Art
    url: https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/env-art
    quote: "A modular kit is a 3D tileset made of modules, environmental meshes designed to snap together in a variety of ways."
  - work: daniel-mcgowan-stages-environment-art
    page: Environment Assets, Modular Assets
    quote: "A good modular set has as few pieces as possible while still being able to achieve a wide range of variety."
  - work: daniel-mcgowan-stages-environment-art
    page: Environment Assets, Modular Assets
    quote: "It is also good practice to have your modular pieces built to a grid for easy snapping and to have a variety of incremented pieces."
