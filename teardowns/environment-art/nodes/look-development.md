kind: process
plain: "Setting the look in small test scenes before it spreads to every level: materials, props and light side by side. A material showcase puts every material in a grid under several kinds of light. A beauty corner brings one small area of a real level to the target quality once its layout is unlikely to change."
does: "Catches assets that do not match each other or the lighting while they are still cheap to fix, and gives the team one place to compare work and agree the standard."
ask: "Do all our materials and props hold together, under every light the game uses?"
trouble: "An asset that looks right only in its own preview and breaks under the game's light; props that drift apart in palette, silhouette or level of detail."
