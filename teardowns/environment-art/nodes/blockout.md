---
term: Blockout
aka:
  - greybox
  - graybox
  - blockmesh
  - whitebox
  - grey boxes
  - rough draft of the level
kind: process
plain: "A rough 3D draft of the scene built from simple shapes, with no detail or final art. It settles layout, scale and the big shapes while they are still cheap to change."
does: "Tests layout, distances and the main shapes in the engine before anyone makes final assets. Paintovers and the first art pass start from it."
ask: "Does the scene work in grey, at the right scale, from the game's own camera?"
trouble: "Detail added before the layout is settled, so every change costs rework; no scale figure, so rooms, paths and props end up the wrong size."
