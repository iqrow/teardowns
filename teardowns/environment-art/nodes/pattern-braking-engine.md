---
term: Braking engine
aka:
  - balancing loop
  - thermostat
  - negative feedback loop
  - goal-seeking loop
kind: pattern
plain: A loop that opposes change and pulls the system toward a level, its set point, like a thermostat.
ask: What level does this loop pull toward, and can I move that level?
sources:
  - work: donella-meadows-leverage-points-places-to
    page: Negative feedback loops, leverage point 8
    quote: "Any negative feedback loop needs a goal (the thermostat setting), a monitoring and signaling device to detect excursions from the goal (the thermostat), and a response mechanism (the furnace and/or air conditioner, fans, heat pipes, fuel, etc.)."
  - work: uk-government-office-for-science-an-introductory
    page: Tool 6, Creating a causal loop diagram
    quote: "Balancing loops are stabilising, regulating and stability seeking. They oppose whatever direction of change is imposed on the system."
