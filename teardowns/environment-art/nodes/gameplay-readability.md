---
term: Gameplay readability
aka:
  - playable read
  - combat clarity
  - gameplay layer
kind: concept
plain: The separation between what the player can use, avoid or understand and the decorative environment around it. Value, saturation, lighting and silhouettes carry this separation across the intended camera views.
does: Keeps routes, cover, threats, allies and interactables visible while the world remains rich.
ask: Can the player identify the playable layer at the speed and distance of play?
at: [0.02, 0.7, 0.04]
cues:
  - Characters and interactables separate from the background by value or saturation
  - Walkable routes and cover have readable edges
  - Decorative detail does not merge with threat or objective silhouettes
trouble: "The player loses an enemy, route or interactable in texture noise, darkness or a similar silhouette."
