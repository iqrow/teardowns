---
term: Defense trigger
aka: [defensive pact, mutual protection]
kind: knob
plain: A defensive pact turns an attack on one signatory into an automatic war declaration by the other against the attacker.
does: Creates deterrence while limiting the obligation to wars started by a third party.
ask: Does the trigger fire on a unit attack, a border entry, a city capture, or any formal declaration?
cues: [Shield linked to an aggressor arrow, A notification that the ally has entered the war]
range: Attack-only trigger; attack or border violation; trigger with a response deadline.
trouble: If an incidental border event triggers war, players avoid the pact; if the trigger is too narrow, attackers route around it and a partner can free-ride on the deterrence.
sources:

    quote: "If either of your civilizations is attacked by a third party, the other civilization automatically declares war on the attacker."
  - work: 2k-civ6-manual
    page: 144
    quote: "If a signatory to a Defensive Pact is attacked, the other partner is automatically at war with the attacker."
