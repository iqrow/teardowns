aka: [lump sum, immediate consideration]

does: Makes the payer's commitment visible and makes the recipient's default cost immediate rather than uncertain.
ask: Is the payment refundable if the treaty fails before its benefit starts?

range: Lump-sum gold; resource or city transfer; immediate technology or map.
trouble: If the recipient can cancel after collecting an upfront price, the payer has no outside option except retaliation.
