---
term: Passage rights
aka: [open borders, transit agreement]
kind: knob
plain: A passage clause lets one or both sides move units through another empire without turning entry into an attack; it need not include a resource, map, or defense clause.
does: Makes geography negotiable and creates a visible route for trade, invasion, and inspection.
ask: Is passage reciprocal, where may units stop, and what happens to units when the clause ends?
cues: [A border gate with arrows in one or both directions, Foreign units crossing without a war banner]
range: One-way passage; reciprocal passage; transit only with no stationing.
trouble: Free permanent access can remove border defense and make expiry strand units inside a rival's territory.
