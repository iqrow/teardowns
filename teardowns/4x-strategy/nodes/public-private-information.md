term: Public and private information
aka: [open ledger, secret negotiation]
kind: knob
plain: A game can publish treaty terms and coalition membership, keep talks private, or expose only the clauses needed for enforcement.
does: Controls whether diplomacy is a contest of public coordination, private signaling, or both.
ask: Which facts must be public for other players to respond, and which hidden facts create useful uncertainty?
cues: [A public notice board with treaty rows, A private scroll or locked message window]
range: All terms public; private talks and public signed terms; private terms and public outcomes.
trouble: If commitments are secret but victory effects are public, outsiders cannot distinguish a real coalition from cheap talk; if every conversation is public, signaling loses value.
