---
term: One-time explored map
aka: [map snapshot, exchanged map]
kind: knob
plain: An immediate map exchange reveals the partner's explored territory as a map item with no duration; it does not itself create a continuing sight feed.
does: Prices scouting history while preserving the value of future exploration and surprise.
ask: Does the snapshot reveal terrain only, or ownership and units too?
cues: [A folded map opening once, A timestamp or dated discovery stamp]
range: Terrain only; explored terrain and borders; explored terrain plus known units.
trouble: If ownership, units, and unexplored terrain are conflated, a stale map reads like live intelligence.
