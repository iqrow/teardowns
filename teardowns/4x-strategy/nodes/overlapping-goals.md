term: Overlapping goals
aka: [staggered timers]
kind: concept
plain: Short- and long-term goals overlap, so a near-term finish can make the long-term goal more accessible.
does: Removes natural stopping points, which drives "one more turn".
ask: On how many turns does nothing finish?
sources:

    page: Interesting decisions are persistent and affect the game for a certain amount of time
    quote: "The player’s task is to prioritize and to manage strategies for both near-term and long-term goals, and evolve the short-term goals to make the long-term goal more accessible."
