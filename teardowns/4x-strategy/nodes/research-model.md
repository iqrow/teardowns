---
term: Research model
aka: [how techs are offered]
kind: concept
plain: "How a game presents research: a visible prerequisite tree, a semi-random hand of options, or a tree with action-triggered boosts."
does: Sets how much planning and adaptation research demands.
ask: Can the player plan research many turns ahead, and should they?
variants:
  - label: Model
    items:
      - id: research-tree
        name: Fixed tree
        plain: All technologies and their prerequisites are visible in a tree, so the player can choose a path.
        sources:
          - work: 2k-civ4-manual
            page: The Tech Tree
            quote: "Many techs have “prerequisite technologies” that you must know before you can learn the new technology … The extremely useful Technology Tree displays all of the technologies in the game and how they are interrelated."
      - id: research-cards
        name: Random draw
        plain: Each time research starts, the game offers a semi-random hand of available technologies.
        sources:
          - work: stellaris-paradoxwikis-technology
            page: The card system
            quote: "Whenever the player is prompted to initiate a research project, the game gives them a \"hand of cards\" consisting of a semi-random selection of technologies available for research"
      - id: research-boosts
        name: Boosts
        plain: A fixed tree in which an action gives part of the needed research for a technology.
        sources:
          - work: 2k-civ6-manual
            page: Tribal Villages
            quote: "The village provides your civilization with a free technology (for example, Bronze Working or Masonry), a technology boost (50% of the needed research towards a technology), or even multiple technology boosts."
