---
term: Dough strength and extensibility
aka: [elasticity, a strong dough, slack dough]
kind: concept
plain: Strength is resistance to stretching and shape loss; extensibility is how far dough stretches before tearing.
does: Their balance lets dough retain gas and shape while expanding; poor balance can make loaves flatten or limit expansion.
ask: Does this dough need more strength or more extensibility?
cues: ["A strong dough keeps a tall, smooth shape", "A more extensible sheet stretches thin before tearing"]
at: [0, 0.4, 0]
