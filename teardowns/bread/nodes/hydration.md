---
term: Hydration
aka: [how wet the dough is, water percentage]
kind: knob
plain: The water percentage of dough relative to flour.
does: Shows how wet or stiff dough will be and affects mixing, strength development, folding, shaping and baking.
ask: How much water can this flour take for this bread?
cues: ["A graduated vessel shows the water level", "The dough sample ranges from stiff and shaggy to glossy and slack"]
at: [0, 0.1, 0.1]
range: Around 60% is low hydration; 80% or higher is high hydration.
trouble: A high-hydration dough can be slack and sticky; a low-hydration dough can be stiff.
