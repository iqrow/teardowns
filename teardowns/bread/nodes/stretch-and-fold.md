term: Stretch and fold
aka: [folds, coil folds, turns]
kind: process
plain: Lifting the dough and folding it over itself a few times during bulk fermentation.
does: Strengthens the dough, equalizes its temperature, and lets the baker assess fermentation.
ask: Does the dough need another set of folds, or is it strong enough?
cues: ["Dough holds a taller shape after each set", "Folded layers leave a smooth, rounded top"]
at: [0.45, 0.5, 0.08]
range: Sets commonly spaced about 30 minutes apart during bulk fermentation
