---
term: Poke test
aka: [finger test, finger dent test]
kind: measure
plain: Pressing the proofing dough with a floured finger and watching how the dent springs back.
does: Tells under-proofed, proofed and over-proofed dough apart.
ask: Does the dent fill back slowly and only partly?
cues: ["Springs back slowly and partly: ready", "Springs back at once: under-proofed", "Stays dented: over-proofed"]
at: [0, 0.55, 0.08]
sources:
  - {work: maurizio-leo-how-to-use-the-dough-poke-test, page: article, quote: "Underproofed Tight in basket Little rise and dense; very elastic Springs back quickly"}
  - {work: maurizio-leo-how-to-use-the-dough-poke-test, page: article, quote: "Properly Proofed Relaxed and soft Airy and light, yet still elastic and cohesive Springs back very slowly"}
  - {work: maurizio-leo-how-to-use-the-dough-poke-test, page: article, quote: "Overproofed Excessively soft and over-relaxed Excessively risen; potentially large bubbles with thin membranes; weak dough structure Never springs back"}
