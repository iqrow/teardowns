term: Enrichment
aka: [enriched dough, "butter, eggs, sugar, milk"]
kind: part
plain: Ingredients such as fat, sugar, milk and eggs added to dough to change tenderness, colour, flavour, browning or structure.
does: Fat can inhibit gluten development; sugar can tenderize, brown the crust and slow yeast at high levels; milk promotes tenderness and browning.
ask: Is this a lean or an enriched dough, and how much fat and sugar does it carry?
