term: Staling
aka: [going stale, retrogradation]
kind: process
plain: Starch slowly recrystallising after baking, which firms the crumb as moisture moves out to the crust.
does: Firms the crumb as starch retrogrades and softens the crust as moisture moves from crumb to crust.

at: [0.5, 0.4, 0]
trouble: A firmer crumb and leathery crust as storage continues.
