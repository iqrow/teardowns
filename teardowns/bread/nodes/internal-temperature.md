---
term: Internal temperature
aka: [is it done, doneness]
kind: measure
plain: The temperature at the centre of the loaf, read with a probe thermometer.
does: Confirms the centre has reached a temperature used to judge doneness.
ask: Has the centre reached the temperature for this kind of bread?
cues: [A probe thermometer in the loaf's centre, A hollow sound when the base is tapped]
at: [0, 0.3, 0.02]
range: "Most loaves and rolls about 190 °F (88 °C); dense whole-grain rounds 205–210 °F (96–99 °C)."
