---
term: Salt
aka: [salt percentage]
kind: part
plain: Salt, usually close to 2% of the flour weight.
does: Seasons the bread, tightens the gluten, and slows fermentation.
ask: Is the salt near 2% of the flour, and when does it go in?
cues: ["White crystals sit in a small dish beside the dough", "The baked loaf has an even savoury crust and crumb"]
at: [0, -0.1, 0.1]
range: About 1.8–2% of flour weight.
trouble: Without enough salt, dough is slack and yeast ferments too quickly; too much salt retards yeast and reduces volume.

  - work: king-arthur-baking-salt
    page: "Salt"
    quote: "Salt provides flavor. … Generally, the correct amount of salt in bread dough is 1.8 to 2% of salt based on flour weight (that is, 1.8–2 pounds of salt per 100 pounds of flour). … Salt tightens the gluten structure. … Salt has a retarding effect on the activity of the yeast."
  - work: king-arthur-baking-salt
    page: "Salt"
    quote: "If there is an excess of salt in bread dough, the yeast is retarded to the point that there is a marked reduction in volume."
  - work: king-arthur-baking-salt
