---
term: Windowpane test
aka: [gluten window, membrane test]
kind: measure
plain: Stretching a small piece of dough into a thin sheet to check gluten development.
does: Shows how strong, elastic and developed the dough is.
ask: Does the dough stretch into a thin, translucent sheet?
cues: ["A thin, translucent membrane", Ragged tears when the gluten is under-developed]
at: [0.7, 0.45, 0.1]
sources:
  - work: jeffrey-hamelman-bread-a-bakers-book-of
    page: "ch. 1, p. 9"
    url: https://catalogimages.wiley.com/images/db/pdf/9781119577515.excerpt.pdf
    quote: "Some bakers pull off a small piece of dough and look at the “gluten window” by gently stretching the dough to the thinnest sheet possible (sometimes called the “windowpane test”)."
  - work: king-arthur-baking-what-is-the-windowpane-test
    page: "What is the windowpane test for bread dough?"
    quote: "It’s called the windowpane test, and it lets you actually see with your eyes how strong the dough is. … This may happen almost immediately, or the dough may stretch so far you can see light through it; this is called “achieving the windowpane.”"
