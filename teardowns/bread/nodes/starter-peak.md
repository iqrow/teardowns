---
term: Starter peak
aka: [ripe starter, when the starter is ready]
kind: measure
plain: The point where a fed starter has risen highest, just before it falls, with bubbles throughout.
does: Marks a ripe starter ready for baking.
ask: Has it risen well past its starting level, domed, and begun to flatten?
cues: ["The starter domes at its highest mark", "Bubbles fill the jar before the surface starts to flatten"]
at: [0, 0.75, 0]
trouble: Under-ripe starter has not doubled; over-ripe starter has doubled or tripled and then started to sink.
sources:
  - work: king-arthur-baking-ripe-sourdough-starter-heres
    page: "Ripe sourdough starter: Here's what that means"
    quote: "Starter that is under-ripe has not yet doubled in size, and starter that is over-ripe has doubled or tripled in size and then started to sink again. … It's doubled in volume, and shows signs of just beginning to sag under its own weight."
  - work: king-arthur-baking-feeding-and-maintaining-your
    page: "Feeding and Maintaining Your Sourdough Starter"
    quote: "If you’re looking to bake with ripe starter, remove what you need for your recipe when it has roughly doubled in volume and become bubbly (about 6 to 8 hours), then feed the remaining starter."
