aka: [bread flour, wheat flour]
kind: part
plain: Ground grain, usually wheat, with an endosperm, bran, germ and proteins that can form gluten.
does: Contains endosperm and gluten-forming protein; coarse whole-grain particles can puncture gluten and require longer mixing.
ask: What is its protein, how much of the whole grain does it keep, and how much water does it want?
cues: [Colour from creamy white to speckled brown, Bag label with protein percentage or a type number]
trouble: Dough can become wet, sticky and loose when flour protein is too low.
