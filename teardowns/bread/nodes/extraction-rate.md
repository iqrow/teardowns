term: Extraction rate and ash
aka: [how wholemeal the flour is, flour type number, ash content]
kind: measure
plain: The share of the wheat kernel recovered as flour, described by extraction rate or by the ash left from a flour sample.
does: Higher extraction leaves more bran in the flour; bran can weaken dough properties and reduce gas retention.
ask: Is this a white flour or a high-extraction one, and what is its ash?
cues: [Bran specks in the flour and crumb, Type numbers such as T65 or 550 on European bags]
at: [-0.7, 0.5, 0.42]
range: Efficient mills normally obtain 72–76% extraction; whole-wheat flour is listed at 100%.
