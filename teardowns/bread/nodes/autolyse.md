---
term: Autolyse
aka: [rest before kneading, flour and water rest]
kind: process
plain: A rest of flour and water before salt and leaven are added.
does: Fully hydrates flour and lets gluten bonds develop with little mechanical mixing.
ask: Did the flour and water rest before the full mix, and for how long?
cues: ["A covered bowl holding flour and water", "The dough looks rough before the rest and smoother after it"]
at: [0, 0.2, 0]
range: About 15–30 minutes.
sources:
  - work: jeffrey-hamelman-bread-a-bakers-book-of
    page: "ch. 1, pp. 10–11"
    url: https://catalogimages.wiley.com/images/db/pdf/9781119577515.excerpt.pdf
    quote: "During the rest period, the flour fully hydrates, and the gluten bonds, somewhat magically, continue to develop in spite of the lack of mechanical mixing."
  - work: king-arthur-baking-traveling-crusty-artisan-bread
    page: "Traveling Crusty Artisan Bread"
    quote: "Let the shaggy dough rest, covered, for 15 to 30 minutes so the flour hydrates. … This is key to great bread, and is called “autolyse.” After the rest period, add the salt and yeast and incorporate well"
