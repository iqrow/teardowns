---
term: Preferment
aka: [poolish, biga, sponge, starter dough]
kind: part
plain: A portion of bread dough's flour, water and leavening made several hours or more before the final dough.
does: Slow fermentation adds acidity that strengthens gluten and contributes flavour, while reducing the bulk fermentation time after mixing.
ask: Which type, at what hydration, and how much of the total flour is prefermented?
cues: ["A covered bowl holds a loose, stiff or dough-like pre-ferment", "Bubbles and a fermented aroma show time has passed"]
at: [0.95, 0.25, 0.05]
range: Made several hours or more ahead; it may be stiff, loose or a piece of mixed dough.
variants:
  - label: Type
    items:
      - id: poolish
        name: Poolish
        plain: A liquid preferment made with equal weights of flour and water and a small amount of yeast.
        sources:
          - work: king-arthur-baking-preferment
            page: "Preferment"
            quote: "It is by definition made with equal weights of flour and water (that is, it is 100% hydration), and a small portion of yeast."
      - id: biga
        name: Biga
        plain: A stiff yeasted preferment.
        sources:
          - work: martin-philip-baking-with-preferments
            page: "Baking with preferments"
            quote: "Stiff Yeasted - Biga - Pâte Fermentée … The stiff yeasted preferment (bottom right of the table) perfumes the finished loaf with floral, aromatic qualities that are unrivaled."
      - id: pate-fermentee
        name: Pâte fermentée
        plain: A piece of finished, salted dough saved and added to the next batch.
        why: It counts by being complete dough with salt.
        sources:
          - work: king-arthur-baking-preferment
            page: "Preferment"
            quote: "Being that pâte fermentée is a piece of mixed dough, we note that it therefore contains all the ingredients of finished dough, that is, flour, water, salt, and yeast."
          - work: king-arthur-baking-preferment
            page: "Preferment"
            quote: "If one were to mix a batch of French bread, and once mixed a portion were removed, and added in to a new batch of dough being mixed the next day, the portion that was removed would be the pâte fermentée."
      - id: sponge
        name: Sponge
        plain: A liquid yeasted preferment.
        sources:

            page: "Baking with preferments"
            quote: "Liquid Yeasted - Poolish - Sponge … In addition to its \"nutty\" flavor, poolish can make baguette doughs easier to handle – the preferment boosts extensibility (the ability for dough to stretch)."
      - id: levain-build
        name: Levain build
        plain: A preferment made from flour, water and ripe sourdough starter.

            quote: "Made from flour, water, and ripe sourdough starter, the preferment (aka levain) for our Pain au Levain recipe ferments overnight, building populations of yeast and LAB."
