---
term: Steam
aka: [oven steam, steaming]
kind: knob
plain: Water vapour in the oven during the first part of the bake.
does: Keeps the surface moist and flexible during oven spring, then supports a shiny, crisp crust.
ask: Was there steam in the first minutes, and was it vented for the rest of the bake?
cues: [A shiny crust, Scores that open wide]
at: [0, 1.3, 0]
trouble: Without steam, the scores do not expand well and the crust can become dull and constricted.
sources:
  - work: jeffrey-hamelman-bread-a-bakers-book-of

    quote: "Proper steaming has a profound effect on bread for a number of reasons: It promotes a rich color to the crust and a surface shine on the loaf, and it also increases the volume of the bread."
  - {work: barb-alpern-baking-bread-with-steam, page: article, quote: "Steam is vital during the oven-spring period so that the surface of the loaf remains moist and expands easily."}
