        name: No-knead and folds

        name: Short mix
        plain: A short mechanical mix followed by a long fermentation.
        sources:
          - work: wayne-gisslen-professional-baking-7th-edition

        name: Improved mix
        plain: A compromise between short and intensive mixing, with partial development and a longer fermentation.
        sources:
          - work: san-francisco-baking-institute-improved-mix
            page: "Improved Mix"
            quote: "A compromise between short and intensive mixing, improved mixing produces a flavorful bread with a creamy, open crumb and good shelf life."

            page: "Improved Mix"

      - id: intensive-mix
        name: Intensive mix
        plain: A long mix followed by a short fermentation, developing the dough strongly.
        sources:
          - work: wayne-gisslen-professional-baking-7th-edition
            page: "ch. 7, p. 119"
            quote: "Intensive mix: A yeast dough mixing technique that combines a long mixing period with a short fermentation period."
