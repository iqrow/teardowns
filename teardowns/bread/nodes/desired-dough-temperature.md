---
term: Desired dough temperature
aka: [DDT, water temperature]
kind: knob
plain: The target temperature of dough at the end of mixing, reached by choosing the water temperature.
does: Helps keep fermentation and baking results consistent.
ask: What water temperature brings the dough to its target after mixing?
cues: ["A probe thermometer in the dough at the end of mixing", "The display settles near 24–26 °C"]
at: [0, 0.85, 0]
range: 75–78 °F (24–26 °C) for wheat-based yeast breads.
