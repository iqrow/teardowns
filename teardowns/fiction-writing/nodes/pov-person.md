  - "I, you or she grammar"
  - "Knowledge limited or omniscient"

    items:

        name: First person

      - id: person-second
        name: Second person
        plain: “You”. Rare and intimate, but it tells readers what they do and feel.
        sources:
          - work: bowie-state-understanding-point-of-view
            page: types of points-of-view
            flag: university guide
            quote: "2 nd person in which the narrative is told to “you”"
      - id: person-third-limited
        name: Third person limited
        plain: “She”. An outside view plus one character’s thoughts. A common choice, and the one The Last Hour uses.
        sources:
          - work: bowie-state-understanding-point-of-view
            page: types of points-of-view
            flag: university guide
            quote: "They can be limited in which they only follow one character"
      - id: person-third-omniscient
        name: Third person omniscient
        plain: The narrator moves freely in time and space, enters many minds, and knows what no character knows.
        sources:
          - work: bowie-state-understanding-point-of-view
            page: types of points-of-view
            flag: university guide
            quote: "omniscient in which the narrator knows everything"
