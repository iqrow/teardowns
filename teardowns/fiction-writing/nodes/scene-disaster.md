---
term: Disaster
aka: [setback]
kind: concept
plain: She fails to reach the goal, or wins and makes things worse. Security is in on it.
does: Keeps tension high. Ingermanson warns that simply granting the goal ends the tension.
ask: How does this scene end worse than it began?
cues:
  - "A win that makes things worse"
  - "A scene ending with a problem still pulling forward"
trouble: Scenes end with the problem solved and nothing to pull the reader on.
sources:
  - work: randy-ingermanson-writing-the-perfect-scene
    page: Large-Scale Structure of a Scene; Motivation-Reaction Units
    quote: "A Scene has the following three-part pattern: Goal Conflict Disaster"
