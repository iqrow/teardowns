---
term: Reverse outline
aka: [after-the-fact outline]
kind: process
plain: "An outline made after drafting: one line per part for its main point. Gaps, repeats and weak order show up at once."
does: Makes the draft’s real structure visible.
ask: What does each chapter change?
trouble: Chapters where nothing changes, or the same beat twice.
sources:
  - {work: purdue-owl-reverse-outlining, page: “How to create a reverse outline”, quote: "When you reverse outline, you identify the main ideas from each paragraph of your paper in order to highlight the structure and organization."}
