---
term: Publishing path
aka: [getting published, traditional vs self-publishing]
kind: process
plain: How the manuscript becomes a book in readers’ hands. Friedman’s chart splits the paths by who pays and who controls the rights.
does: Decides the money, control and reach of the finished book.
ask: Who pays, who keeps the rights, and how does the book reach readers?
trouble: Paying for a contract with no clear way to readers.
at: [0.88, 0.2706, -0.1855]
variants:
  - label: Path
    items:
      - id: path-traditional
        name: Traditional
        plain: Usually through an agent. The publisher pays to produce and distribute the book and pays an advance and royalties; you give up some control.
        sources:
          - work: jane-friedman-the-key-book-publishing-paths-2025
            page: Traditional publishing
            quote: "Traditional publishing definition: The publisher takes on the financial risk of publication, whether or not they pay an advance."
      - id: path-self
        name: Self-publishing
        plain: "You are the publisher: you pay the costs, hire the help, and keep the rights and control."
        sources:
          - work: jane-friedman-the-key-book-publishing-paths-2025
            page: Self-publishing
            quote: "Self-publishing. I define this as publishing on your own, where you essentially start your own publishing company, and directly hire and manage all help needed."
      - id: path-hybrid
        name: Hybrid
        plain: The publisher’s name goes on the book, but you pay for publication. Packages and costs vary widely.
        sources:
          - work: jane-friedman-the-key-book-publishing-paths-2025
            page: Hybrid Publishers
            quote: "Hybrid publishers work like traditional publishers in that they put their name on your book and become the publisher of record."
sources:
  - work: jane-friedman-the-key-book-publishing-paths-2025
    page: publishing-path chart
    quote: "This chart’s logic is driven primarily by how the money works and how and where the work is sold."
