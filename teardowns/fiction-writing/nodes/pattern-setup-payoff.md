---
term: Setup and payoff
aka: [promise and payoff, Chekhov’s gun]
kind: pattern
plain: Something placed early is used later. The early placement creates an expectation; the later use fulfils it in a way that still surprises.
ask: What did I set up, and where does it pay off?
sources:
  - work: anton-chekhov-letter-to-a-s-lazarev-gruzinsky
    page: Letter 707, 1 Nov 1889
    flag: Russian primary text; translated fact
    quote: "Нельзя ставить на сцене заряженное ружье, если никто не имеет в виду выстрелить из него."
  - work: brandon-sanderson-2025-guide-to-plot-lecture-2
    page: "The Plot Framework: Promise, Progress, and Payoff"
    quote: "A payoff is more than just an ending—it’s “a surprising but fulfilling answer to your promise.”"
