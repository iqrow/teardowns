---
term: Goal
aka: [scene goal]
kind: concept
plain: "What the viewpoint character wants in this scene. It must be specific and clear: get the list to security."
does: Gives the scene a direction and a test of success.
ask: What exactly does she want in the next ten pages?
cues:
  - "A viewpoint character pursuing a clear task"
  - "A visible test of success"
trouble: A vague wish, such as “she wants to feel safe”.
sources:
  - work: randy-ingermanson-writing-the-perfect-scene
    page: Large-Scale Structure of a Scene; Motivation-Reaction Units
    quote: "A Scene has the following three-part pattern: Goal Conflict Disaster"
